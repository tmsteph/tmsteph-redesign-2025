# Human Scale San Diego — Organic Launch Campaign v0.1

**Goal:** earn attention by publishing useful local work, not by repeatedly promoting a philosophy.

Primary landing page:

https://tmsteph.com/human-scale/san-diego/

Primary funnel:

**useful local result → San Diego page → Audit / Evidence / Lab → contribution or local experiment**

---

# Positioning

Human Scale is not being launched as:

- a political party;
- a lifestyle brand;
- a wellness program;
- an anti-technology movement;
- a nostalgia movement;
- a finished ideology.

The launch line is:

> **Human Scale is a public experiment in making modern life work better for actual humans. We publish the doctrine, the evidence against it, and small real-world tests. San Diego is the first local laboratory.**

---

# Content Rule

Every promotional post should contain at least one of:

- a useful map;
- a surprising measurement;
- a local receipt;
- a before/after result;
- a failed experiment;
- an evidence correction;
- a specific question;
- a tool someone can use immediately.

Avoid posts whose only information is:

> Human Scale exists. Please click.

---

# Post 1 — The Audit

Hook:

> **I built a five-minute audit for a question I can’t stop thinking about: how much does your everyday environment actually support human life?**

Body direction:

Explain that the tool looks at movement, nature/living systems, community, time, agency, place, security, and meaning.

Important caveat:

> The score is deliberately provisional. It is not a scientific diagnostic. I want people to tell me where the questions are wrong, culturally narrow, ableist, double-counted, or missing something important.

CTA:

Take the Audit, then challenge one question.

Best destination:

https://tmsteph.com/human-scale/audit/

# Post 2 — The Doctrine Corrected Itself

Hook:

> **I wrote a philosophy about “human-scale” living, then started auditing the science behind my own favorite claims. Some of them lost.**

Body direction:

Briefly explain:

- “humans need small communities” became too broad;
- digital relationships were given more legitimacy;
- “return to nature” became ecological dependence + site-specific living infrastructure;
- food forests became experiments rather than default recommendations;
- urban-soil contamination became an explicit correction.

CTA:

Read an Evidence Audit and find the next claim that should lose.

Destination:

https://tmsteph.com/human-scale/evidence/

- hold time;

# Post 5 — Quiet Technology

Hook:

> **I don’t want less technology. I want technology that finishes the task and gives my attention back.**

Body direction:

Describe the one-week Quiet Technology experiment:

- keep the service;
- remove nonessential notifications;
- remove badges/feed shortcuts;
- use direct access where possible;
- measure missed information, useful tasks, checking and frustration.

CTA:

Run it for one week and report what actually gets worse as well as what improves.

Destination:

https://tmsteph.com/human-scale/lab/

# HN / Technical Community Angle

Do not pitch Human Scale as politics first.

Best angles:

- a living doctrine that publishes falsification conditions;
- a website that versions philosophical claims like software;
- Evidence Audits that cause public doctrine diffs;
- open-source civic experiments;
- an Audit that intentionally invites criticism of its own scoring model;
- quiet technology / interoperability / repairability;
- using AI to accelerate a philosophy while requiring evidence labels.

Potential Show HN title:

> **Show HN: Human Scale — a versioned doctrine that publishes the evidence against itself**

Post structure:

1. one paragraph explaining the premise;
2. point to the public diff/corrections;
3. state what is deliberately unfinished;
4. ask for methodological/evidence criticism;
5. link Evidence Atlas before asking for broad agreement.

Avoid ideological bait.

# Reddit / Community Forum Rule

Never paste the same generic promotional post across many communities.

Instead:

- answer existing questions where Human Scale material is directly useful;
- post a tool/result only where self-promotion rules allow it;
- disclose authorship;
- participate in comments;
- invite critique;
- do not DM nonresponders;
- do not repeat outreach to quiet addresses/groups as a follow-up tactic.

The project already has a standing outreach ethic: silence is not consent to repeated follow-up.

---

# Local Outreach Angle

When contacting a library, civic group, maker group, university, neighborhood group, or local publication, do not lead with:

> I wrote a doctrine.

Lead with:

> We’re running a small public experiment on [third places / shade / civic navigation / human time] and publishing the results openly. Would this be useful to your community, or is there a better existing effort we should join instead?

This positions Human Scale as a contributor rather than a competitor for attention.

# Metrics

## Primary

- completed public receipts;
- outside contributors;
- evidence challenges;
- doctrine corrections caused by outside input;
- experiments run by someone else;
- institutions responding to a specific pilot;
- repeat visitors to Lab/Evidence pages;
- Audit completions where measurable.

## Secondary

- page views;
- shares;
- followers;
- mentions;
- newsletter or RSS subscriptions if added later.

## Anti-metric

Do not optimize engagement if the content becomes:

- more inflammatory;
- more partisan than the doctrine;
- more certain than the evidence;
- more personally targeted;
- less useful;
- harder to falsify.

---

# Paid Advertising Gate

Do not pay to amplify Human Scale until at least one organic message consistently produces meaningful downstream action.

Before spending money, identify:

- which landing page converts attention into useful engagement;
- which message produces Audit completion, Lab use, or contribution;
- what a meaningful conversion is worth;
- which audiences are contextual/topic audiences rather than sensitive-person targeting;
- a small budget cap;
- stop conditions.

The first paid test, if any, should promote a useful public tool or local result—not political persuasion.

---

# Launch Standard

**Every advertisement should leave the audience with something useful even if they never become a Human Scale supporter.**

That keeps promotion aligned with the doctrine itself.
