Human Scale

Modern life gives us amazing tools. It can also make it harder to sleep, move, connect, have time, and feel in control.

Modern life is powerful. It should also feel human.

What is Human Scale?

It is a simple idea: keep what modern life does well, and make daily life fit people better.

Keep the good.

Medicine, science, computers, energy, trade, cities, and useful machines.

Bring back what people need.

Time, movement, sleep, nature, close relationships, useful skills, and places to belong.

Fix systems that get in the way.

At home, at work, in neighborhoods, in technology, and in government.

A simple path

You do not need to read a book. Start with these three steps.

1

Understand the idea

A short, plain-language introduction.
2

Check your daily life

See where life supports you and where it adds friction.
3

Change one thing

Pick one small change you can try this week.

That is enough to begin.

Human Scale is much bigger than these three steps. But the deeper work should be there when you want it — not in your way when you first arrive.
