Human Scale is a public experiment in making modern life work better for actual humans. The doctrine is versioned. The evidence against it is published. The useful parts are meant to leave the website and enter real life.

Do not join because you agree. Find one claim that fails reality — or one small thing worth testing.

What Human Scale is trying to hold together

Keep modern medicine, science, computers, global knowledge, abundant energy, specialized institutions, cities, markets, and powerful technology where they work.

Recover more human time, agency, health, relationships, useful competence, ecological durability, intelligible institutions, public space, childhood, care, and the ability to participate in the systems around us.

Human Scale is not “small good, big bad.” Scale has to justify itself. The right scale follows the problem.

Advertising rules

Useful before persuasive. Promote a tool, result, map, experiment, correction, or question.
Disclose authorship and sponsorship. No fake grassroots support.
No repeated unwanted outreach. Silence is not an invitation to keep contacting someone.
No sensitive-vulnerability targeting. Do not target fear, illness, religion, family crisis, financial distress, or political vulnerability.
No fake certainty. Link evidence for empirical claims and label values as values.
Earn paid amplification. Do not spend meaningfully until an organic artifact produces useful downstream action.

What counts as growth?

Not only page views. Better outcomes are: an outside evidence challenge, a doctrine correction, an experiment run by someone else, a useful local receipt, a contributor, a public institution responding to a concrete finding, or a tool that keeps being used after the launch post disappears.

If growth makes the project more inflammatory, more partisan, more certain than the evidence, or less useful, it is the wrong growth.
