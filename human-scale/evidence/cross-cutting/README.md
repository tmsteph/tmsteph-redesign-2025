# Human Scale — Cross-Cutting Evidence Audits

**Current frontier — August 2026**

The first 17 chapter audits are complete.

The next research phase tests assumptions that recur across many chapters and can remain hidden when each subject is studied in isolation.

## Completed

### 01. Disability & Accessibility

- Source: [accessibility/audit.md](accessibility/audit.md)
- Public page: [accessibility/index.html](accessibility/index.html)

Core correction:

> **Accessibility is not a concession to exceptional humans. Human variation is part of the scale.**

Key doctrine effects:

- independence means control, not absence of support;
- assistive technology can be paradigmatic Human Scale technology;
- digital participation can be first-class community and civic access;
- walkability alone is not accessible mobility;
- calm technology should become configurable clarity;
- disability, chronic illness, neurotype, age, and temporary impairment belong in the baseline model of the human user.

### 02. Aging & End-of-Life

- Source: [aging-end-of-life/audit.md](aging-end-of-life/audit.md)
- Public page: [aging-end-of-life/index.html](aging-end-of-life/index.html)

Core correction:

> **A humane civilization should remain inhabitable across the life course.**

Key doctrine effects:

- chronological age is not capability;
- healthy aging is about functional ability and well-being, not appearing young;
- replace “aging in place” ideology with aging with choice of place;
- integrate health and social care as needs become more complex;
- support autonomy without requiring zero risk or unsupported independence;
- treat long-term care and palliative care as normal infrastructure;
- death is part of life, not proof that medicine, family, or the person failed.

### 03. Transportation & Mobility

- Source: [transportation-mobility/audit.md](transportation-mobility/audit.md)
- Public page: [transportation-mobility/index.html](transportation-mobility/index.html)

Core correction:

> **The purpose of mobility is access.**

Key doctrine effects:

- replace walkability as master metric with usable access;
- walking, wheeling, cycling, transit, cars, delivery, and remote access are mechanisms rather than moral identities;
- apply Safe System thinking so ordinary mistakes are less likely to become fatal;
- count door-to-door human time, waiting, transfers, parking, and uncertainty;
- reduce compulsory car dependence without pretending cars have no legitimate role;
- evaluate transportation with land use and destination geography;
- preserve multimodal fallback where feasible.

### 04. Administrative Burden

- Source: [administrative-burden/audit.md](administrative-burden/audit.md)
- Public page: [administrative-burden/index.html](administrative-burden/index.html)

Core correction:

> **The goal is not zero bureaucracy. It is justified procedure with the lowest human burden compatible with the function.**

Key doctrine effects:

- measure the full journey: learning, compliance, waiting, travel, uncertainty, money, correction, and psychological cost;
- make the Bureaucracy Receipt a standard Lab instrument;
- distinguish protective procedure from administrative waste;
- digitization is not automatically simplification;
- preserve human escalation and multiple channels for consequential services;
- visible case status is institutional infrastructure;
- use AI on explanation, translation, routing, search, and prefill before opaque rights-critical judgment;
- measure burden on staff and institutions as well as the public.

### 05. Auditing the Human Scale Audit

- Source: [audit-calibration/audit.md](audit-calibration/audit.md)
- Public page: [audit-calibration/index.html](audit-calibration/index.html)

Core correction:

> **If the total score creates more false certainty than insight, Human Scale should be willing to delete it.**

Calibration sequence:

1. adversarial content review;
2. cognitive interviews;
3. accessibility/usability testing;
4. pilot sample and test–retest;
5. fairness / differential-item stress test;
6. sensitivity to real Lab interventions;
7. explicit decision about whether a total score deserves to survive.

The Audit remains a private reflection tool, not a diagnostic or validated index.

### 06. Resilience & Disaster

- Source: [resilience-disaster/audit.md](resilience-disaster/audit.md)
- Public page: [resilience-disaster/index.html](resilience-disaster/index.html)

Core correction:

> **Resilience is a property of a network, not a synonym for decentralization.**

Key doctrine effects:

- start with essential functions and recovery;
- use nested household, community, regional, state, national, and larger capacity;
- invest in redundancy selectively rather than worshiping slack;
- keep mutual aid and formal emergency response complementary;
- make disability and medical power dependence central to planning;
- keep strong grids and local resilience complementary;
- diversify food and communication systems rather than maximize locality;
- include administrative recovery burden and after-action learning.

## Cross-cutting rule

A cross-cutting audit should change multiple chapters or reveal that an assumed “human” user was narrower than the population the doctrine claims to serve.

If an audit only creates another page without changing how Human Scale thinks, it has failed.
