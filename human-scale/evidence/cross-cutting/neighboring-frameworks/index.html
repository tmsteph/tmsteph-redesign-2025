Intellectual ancestry & disagreement

Human Scale & Neighboring Frameworks

Human Scale shares ideas with older political, ecological, economic, urban, and philosophical traditions. The question is not whether every ingredient is new. It is whether the synthesis is coherent, testable, and useful.

A philosophy becomes easier to evaluate when it stops acting as though it invented every idea it uses.

Closest family resemblance

Capability approach

Strong overlap: judge systems by what people are actually able to be and do, not resources alone.

Challenge: Are Human Scale metrics measuring real capability or attractive proxies?

UNDP Human Development ↗

Subsidiarity

Strong precedent for assigning functions to the lowest competent level while larger institutions support what smaller ones cannot do.

Challenge: Is the function really at the lowest competent level — with support rather than abandonment?

Quadragesimo Anno ↗

E.F. Schumacher

Human-scale production, meaningful work, ecological purpose, and skepticism of gigantism are clear ancestors.

Difference: Human Scale now requires small systems to justify themselves too.

Political neighbors

Liberalism

Rights, pluralism, conscience, due process, privacy, and limits on arbitrary power.

Challenge: When does designing for flourishing become paternalism?

Conservatism

Continuity, inherited institutions, family, local knowledge, and caution toward abstract redesign.

Challenge: What evolved function will a clean reform accidentally destroy?

Socialism / social democracy

Worker voice, care, bargaining power, ownership, inequality, public goods, social protection.

Challenge: How meaningful is exit when power and ownership are highly unequal?

Libertarianism

Voluntary association, competition, entrepreneurship, decentralization, exit, and skepticism of coercion.

Challenge: Is coercion actually necessary for the outcome being pursued?

Ecological futures

Degrowth

Shares skepticism of GDP and consumption as complete measures, plus care, time, ecological limits, and sufficiency.

Difference: Human Scale is comfortable with selective abundance in clean energy, housing, medicine, computation, knowledge, and assistive technology.

Ecomodernism

Shares advanced clean energy, science, dense/efficient systems, high-productivity technology, and refusal to romanticize poverty.

Challenge: Is Human Scale valuing local participation more than measurable ecological outcomes?

Ecomodernism ↗

Solarpunk

Shares imagination of gardens + computers, repair, community, clean energy, ecological integration, and optimistic future-building.

Difference: aesthetics are imagination, not evidence. A humane system does not have to look village-like or green.

Implementation neighbors

Strong Towns

Small bets, local feedback, financially resilient development, human-oriented streets, and skepticism of giant irreversible plans.

Challenge: Can we test this cheaply before committing at scale?

Strong Towns Approach ↗

Effective Altruism

Evidence, comparison, opportunity cost, explicit tradeoffs, and willingness to revise.

Challenge: What else could these resources do, and would that produce more of what we claim to value?

Well-being / Doughnut-style economics

Shares rejection of GDP as the sole objective and interest in social foundations plus ecological constraints.

Human Scale contribution: connect macro goals to lived capability, time, access, and burden receipts.

What may actually be distinctive

Capability + embodiment

Diverse bodies and environments, not abstract citizens or consumers.

Scale as empirical variable

Small and large systems both have to justify themselves.

Selective abundance

Advanced capability without technological totalism.

Pluralism + rights floor

Community, family, spirituality, and localism stop where coercion and rights violations begin.

Versioned doctrine

Evidence audits and public revisions are part of the philosophy itself.

Receipts

Translate values into time, access, burden, capability, resilience, and observable local outcomes.

Personal + institutional bridge

Field Guide and Program distinguish adaptation from system redesign.

The village + the computer

Reject the forced choice between advanced capability and embodied, relational, ecological life.

No single ingredient is obviously unique. The synthesis has to earn its own name through usefulness.

Project corrections

Human Scale should acknowledge intellectual ancestors openly; use capability language more precisely; name subsidiarity as a major precedent; distinguish itself from “small is beautiful” with a two-way scale test; distinguish itself from degrowth through selective abundance; let ecomodernism pressure-test romantic localism; borrow Strong Towns-style small bets; keep opportunity cost visible; and use solarpunk for imagination without turning the aesthetic into evidence.

Result of Cross-Cutting Audit 09

Human Scale is not intellectually isolated, and it should stop behaving as though every ingredient is new. Its strongest claim to distinctiveness is a synthesis: capability and embodiment, pluralism and rights, selective abundance, empirical scale, technological agency, public falsification, and practical receipts connecting the philosophy to ordinary life.
