Evidence & uncertainty

Human Scale Evidence Atlas

What do we actually know, what do we merely suspect, what do we value, and what are we choosing politically?

The audit counts only if it is allowed to make our favorite idea weaker.

The method

Human Scale contains many empirical claims and many non-empirical commitments. The Atlas breaks them apart so they cannot borrow certainty from one another.

Strong evidence

Consistent support

Substantial support for a narrowly stated claim; implementation can still fail.

Moderate

Credible direction

Causality, magnitude, population, dose, or implementation remains uncertain.

Hypothesis

Worth testing

A plausible mechanism that must remain vulnerable to domain-specific failure.

Value / proposal

A choice

Evidence constrains it. Science alone does not decide what society ought to value.

Research rule: evidence → inference → values → political mechanism. Then state what result would make us change our minds.

Corrections that now define Human Scale

Novel ≠ harmful

Mismatch is a question to test.

Small ≠ good

Scale follows the function.

Local ≠ sustainable

Food, energy, and community are judged by outcomes.

Open ≠ automatically free

Security, support, complexity, and exit all count.

Belonging ≠ captivity

Rights, privacy, and practical exit are part of care and community.

Engagement ≠ harm

Measure regret, displacement, privacy, outcomes, and user intent.

Prevention ≠ always better

Prevention and treatment both require evidence.

AI exposure ≠ job loss

Tasks, jobs, transformation, automation, and displacement are different outcomes.

What would make the project lose?

Supposed mismatch effects disappear under stronger designs.
Favored smaller or local systems underperform without gains in agency, resilience, rights, or accountability.
Favored interventions create larger safety, maintenance, exclusion, financial, or ecological burdens than benefits.
People with meaningful choice repeatedly prefer arrangements the doctrine predicts should be worse while achieving equal or better outcomes.
A policy aimed at vulnerable people predictably narrows their real choices.
AI, health, education, or media systems outperform Human Scale's preferred alternatives on real outcomes without hidden dependency or rights costs.
Better evidence contradicts an evidence grade published here.
A doctrine that cannot lose an argument is not learning.
