Growth without manipulation

Human Scale should advertise through useful artifacts—tools, maps, evidence corrections, experiments, implementation receipts, and questions worth answering.

Every advertisement should leave the audience with something useful even if they never become a Human Scale supporter.

The project explicitly rejects fake grassroots support, repeated unwanted outreach, deceptive scarcity, hidden sponsorship, harassment, doxxing, and sensitive-vulnerability targeting.

What maturity would look like

The project is not mature when it has enough pages.

It becomes mature when people other than its author can understand it, challenge it, test it, improve it, and use it without asking permission.
