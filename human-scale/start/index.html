Step 1 of 3 · Understand the idea

Modern life should work for people.

Human Scale is about keeping the best parts of modern life while making everyday life healthier, calmer, more connected, and easier to understand.

We gained a lot.

Modern life gave us medicine, electricity, machines, fast travel, computers, clean water, global knowledge, and many kinds of freedom.

Human Scale is not about giving those things up.

But daily life can still feel wrong.

Many people spend too much time sitting, driving, scrolling, working, waiting, or dealing with systems they do not understand.

It can be hard to get enough sleep, see friends, spend time outside, cook, move your body, or feel like your time is your own.

A modern life can be advanced and still be hard on people.

The idea is simple.

Keep what helps.

Science, medicine, technology, energy, trade, and large systems when they work well.

Make room for human needs.

Sleep, movement, nature, close relationships, time, meaning, useful skills, and places to belong.

Change bad defaults.

If a home, job, street, app, or public system keeps making life harder, ask how it could work better.

Human Scale asks a few basic questions.

Does this help people live healthy lives?
Does it give people time and real choices?
Does it help people connect with other people and the living world?
Can normal people understand it, use it, and leave it if they need to?
Is the size of the system helping, or just making it harder to control?

There is no rule that small is always good or new is always bad. We test what works.

Now look at your own life.

The next step is a short check. It is not a test and there is no perfect score.
