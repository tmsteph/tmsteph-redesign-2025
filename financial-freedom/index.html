Why Can't Everyone Be Financially Independent?

I work hard. So do millions of other people. Why does financial security remain so difficult—and what would it take to make freedom ordinary?

Wealth is not merely money. It is access to the necessities, opportunities and time that make a good life possible.

Money versus real wealth

A high salary means little if housing, healthcare and transportation consume it all. Real prosperity means reliable access to useful goods and services, financial resilience, and freedom to choose how we spend our time.

Why hard work isn't enough

Pay depends on more than effort: scarcity of skills, bargaining power, ownership, institutions and market demand all matter. Workers sell hours. Owners may earn from assets and systems, but also carry costs and risk. Neither path guarantees security.

What keeps us from universal independence?

Physical scarcity, unequal ownership, high essential costs, and uneven distribution of productivity gains. Automation can increase what society produces, but technology alone does not decide who benefits.

What if we shared the tools?

Open-source software, accessible computing, education, cooperatives and broadly distributed ownership can help more people participate in production. Housing supply, public infrastructure, healthcare and fair institutions matter too. No single technology is a complete economic system.

Start with financial breathing room

For an individual, the sequence is practical: cover essentials, create a cash buffer, raise effective earnings, reduce avoidable recurring costs, and build productive assets carefully. A business is not passive income until revenue reliably exceeds operating costs and the owner's labor.

What is the economy for?

If food, housing, healthcare and basic security were reliably accessible, many of us would still create, care, invent, teach and build. Financial independence is not necessarily the end of work; it is freedom from having to accept any work just to survive.

My question is not simply how to become rich. It's how to build technology and institutions that make a dignified life accessible to everyone.
