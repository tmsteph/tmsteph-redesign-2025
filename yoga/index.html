Begin anywhere

Your first 12 minutes

2 min · Arrive

Sit or stand comfortably. Breathe naturally, notice your surroundings, and soften your shoulders.

6 min · Move

Try gentle neck and shoulder mobility, Cat–Cow, and an easy standing forward fold with bent knees. Move without forcing.

4 min · Rest

Lie down or sit supported. Notice your breathing and how you feel. There is nothing to achieve.

Adapt or skip anything painful. Stop if dizzy or unwell. This is peer learning, not individualized medical advice or certified instruction.

One month of beginnings

The 30-day journey

Days 1–7 · Show up

Practice 5–12 minutes daily. Explore comfortable breathing, rest, and gentle mobility.

Days 8–14 · Find a rhythm

Learn foundational standing shapes and optional modified Sun Salutations at your own pace.

Days 15–21 · Go deeper

Explore balance, mindful movement, meditation, and the history and philosophy of yoga.

Days 22–30 · Share

Reflect on what helped, build a repeatable routine, and share your experience without claiming expertise.

Sunrise ritual

Greet the morning

As your practice develops, explore Surya Namaskar (Sun Salutations), a flowing sequence often practiced in the morning. Begin with accessible modifications, rest when needed, and let consistency matter more than intensity.

Yoga also has rich South Asian spiritual and philosophical traditions. This journey aims to approach those roots with curiosity and respect, while making space for each person's beliefs.

From learning to teaching

Build something we can share.

  1. Practice: develop a personal routine and journal what works.
  2. Study: learn anatomy, safe modifications, breathwork, philosophy, and teaching ethics from qualified sources.
  3. Collaborate: invite experienced teachers to review beginner resources.
  4. Create: publish free, accessible lessons, open-source animations, and eventually immersive practice spaces.
  5. Teach responsibly: pursue supervised training before offering formal instruction.

This page is the beginning, not a claim of mastery. The goal is a welcoming place where we can learn together.
