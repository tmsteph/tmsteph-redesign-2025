Tradition, interpretation & science

Chakra systems have diverse roots in South Asian spiritual traditions. The familiar seven-color rainbow, crystal and essential-oil correspondences are largely modern interpretations, not one universal ancient canon. Yoga, breathing, music, and scent can affect experience and well-being, but there is no established scientific evidence that crystals or oils activate measurable chakra energy centers. Essential oils should be diluted appropriately, not ingested, and used cautiously around children and pets.

Explore with curiosity. Notice what helps you feel grounded, connected, or attentive—without treating this map as a medical diagnosis.
